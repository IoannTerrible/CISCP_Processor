CISC-16 instruction set

      
    

    

      Registers: A and R0..R7 (8 bit), SI, DI, BP, SP (16 bit), FLAGS = Z C N V I.
      Operands: imm8 / imm16 (number, 'c' or label), [addr] direct, [Rn] zero page indirect,
      [addr+Rn] indexed, [SI] register indirect, [BP+disp] / [BP-disp] based.
      Directives: .org addr, .db v, "text", .dw word, label, .ds count, .equ NAME, value.
      Ports: OUT 0 number, OUT 1 character, OUT 2/3 timer period, OUT 4 timer control,
      IN A, 1 input, IN A, 6 input status. Interrupt vectors live at 0xFFF0 + 2 × IRQ, R6 is the REP count, R7 the high byte of MUL / DIV.
